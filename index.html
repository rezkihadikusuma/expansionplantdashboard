Detail Engineering Design

A guide to engineering document and drawing issue status codes.

IFC

Issued for Construction

The document has been issued for use as a reference during field construction.

IFA

Issued for Approval

The document has been submitted to the relevant parties for review and approval.

IFR

Issued for Review

The document has been submitted for review and comments; it is not yet a construction reference.

IFI

Issued for Information

The document is issued for information or reference, not for approval or construction.

Code definitions and sequences may follow the project's document control procedure. Refer to the status on the applicable transmittal or document revision.
